Arrow Rush

Version {{ appVersion() }}

The Game

Arrow Rush is a fast-paced, minimalist directional puzzle game built to test your spatial logic, timing, and forward-planning. Your goal is straightforward: clear every arrow from the board along its flight path without colliding into other arrows or obstacles.

Arrow Mechanics & Directions

Every arrow on the grid is oriented in a cardinal direction and accented with distinct, readable colors:

  • Purple Arrows: Facing upward with vertical clearance trajectories.
  • Blue Arrows: Oriented rightward, navigating horizontal lanes.
  • Green Arrows: Pointing downward requiring sequenced unblocking.
  • Red Arrows: Directed leftward through intersecting choke points.
  • Yellow Arrows: Pivotal connectors across multi-step solutions.

Game Modes & Features

  • Handcrafted Level Map: Over 100 progressive levels with expanding grid sizes and intricate obstacle layouts.
  • Daily Challenge: A unique daily puzzle that resets every 24 hours to test your speed and build winning streaks.
  • Themes & Customization: Unlockable board themes and visual skins to tailor the aesthetic to your style.
  • Tactile Haptic Feedback: Responsive physical sensation for every arrow launch, collision, and victory.

Offline-First & Fair Play

Arrow Rush is fully playable offline. All puzzle progress, best scores, and unlocked customizations are saved directly on your device so you can play anytime without an active network connection.

To maintain your game state and custom preferences, Arrow Rush stores the following data locally on your device:
